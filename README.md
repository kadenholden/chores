# Duties

Kaden's daily duties for the Supreme Leader's house: a phone app you add to the Home Screen.

- **Today**: duties in three blocks (morning reset, through the day, night close-down) plus as-needed jobs like laundry. Tap to tick.
- **Streak and ranks**: a day counts when every duty due that day is done. Miss one and the streak resets. Day 15 is the "prove her wrong" milestone.
- **Inspection**: hand her the phone and she stamps the day Approved, Passable or Redo, with an optional note.
- **Report**: makes a report card picture to send her.
- **Standards**: her rules, editable duties, start date, and backup/restore.

Everything is stored only on the phone (browser storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Install: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Files: `index.html` (the whole app), `sw.js` (offline support), `manifest.webmanifest` and icons.
